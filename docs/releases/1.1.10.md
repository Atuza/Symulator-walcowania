# 1.1.10 - P460NL2 cylinder cut-length calibration

Date: 2026-10-30.

P460NL2 cylinders can use the empirical **−3.989383 mm** cut-length adjustment from the five
confirmed cylinder trials in report revision 2. The option has its own visible switch and status;
it is separate from the neutral-axis k and the springback factor. Neither concentric nor eccentric
cones, other profiles or other materials receive the correction.

The profile operates at t = 9.2–10.0 mm without flat-end allowance or manual k. It shows the trial
range (outer circumference 6916–7548 mm, the same machine/process, program setpoint P = 55 bar)
and warns if a circumference extrapolates beyond it. Values outside the thickness range, with
allowances or manual k retain their geometric cut model and show the reason.

Diameter, radius, circumference and cut-length edits use the same forward/inverse calculation.
For inner Ø2380 and t10, the corrected blank is **7504.42 mm**, versus geometric **7508.41 mm**.
The drawing, PDF, DXF and stock mass use that corrected cut length. The geometric ring remains
separate and the illustrative 3D view reaches full closure at the target radius.

Old saved jobs/templates/history and version-1 or legacy QR links keep the geometric model until
the operator enables the option. New version-2 links retain the choice while preserving the QR
size budget. The calibration comes from measured cut/circumference/thickness observations; it
does not infer pressure settings, a physical elongation law or a guaranteed tolerance.

Regression coverage includes all surfaces, the length inverse, manual-k/allowance/thickness gates,
both cone types and legacy shapes, old/new persisted inputs and links, synchronized exports,
Enter commits and invalid-edit rejection. Publication requires the complete local checks and
terminal CI and Pages deployment for the published commit.

Local verification: 363 unit tests, 20 desktop/phone Chromium browser tests, lint, TypeScript,
Prettier, production build and a production dependency audit passed. A4 and A3 exports were
rendered and checked. Longer calibration notes wrap in the result/PDF tables. Mobile panels retain
their content height so the results do not cover the drawing's clickable dimensions. iPad WebKit
and the complete browser matrix run in CI before the release is reported as deployed.

The v1.2 machine-program editor remains deferred. Source reports, manufacturer documents and
machine photos are not published with the application.
