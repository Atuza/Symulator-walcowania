# 1.1.6 — Readable development of shallow cones

Date: 2026-10-29.

A nearly cylindrical cone could appear as a tiny line because the drawing fitted both the sheet
and guide lines leading to its distant virtual apex. A 2405/2398 mm cone, 3000 mm high, has an
opening angle of about 0.42 degrees and a development radius above one million millimetres, despite
the cut sheet being about 7555.51 by 3006.90 mm on the middle surface.

The SVG and PDF development now frame the sheet itself. The remote apex guide lines and the opening-
angle annotation are omitted. The generator-length dimension moves to the opposite side from the
sheet-height dimension, preventing those values from overlapping on shallow tapers. True cut curves,
edge lengths and editable development radii remain available.
Long radius values also fit their PDF result columns without running into the next value.

Cone calculations, mass, allowances, the 3D model, DXF contours and installed-machine geometry remain
unchanged. The v1.2 machine-program editor remains deferred.

Verification covers 256 tests, including shallow cones on all reference surfaces, a more extreme
near-cylinder, apex cones, flat allowances, readable screen/PDF framing and separated labels.
Lint, TypeScript, formatting and the production build are checked before publication, with browser,
rendered PDF and DXF comparison checks. The tagged release records CI and deployment receipts.
