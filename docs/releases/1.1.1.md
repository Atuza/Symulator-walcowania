# 1.1.1 — Eccentric cone and normal wall thickness

Date: 2026-10-28.

## Changes

- Separate Eccentric cone tile, with centre offset and Flat side controls. The concentric Cone
  tile keeps its closed-form solver. Legacy non-zero-offset cone links, templates and saved inputs
  select the new tile and preserve dimensions.
- Replace mean-slope thickness conversion with constant offsets along the local surface normal.
  Inner, neutral and outer walls are trimmed by the same parallel end planes. Develop the neutral
  surface isometrically with adaptive numerical refinement targeting 0.01 mm for contour and edge
  lengths. Reject inward singularities and failure to converge.
- Derive the minimum inner curvature for process checks and automatic neutral factor. Label derived
  end diameters as perimeter / π and show the range of wall separation in an end plane.
- Use the same exact wall construction in the solid 3D preview and side drawing. Update Polish and
  English shape references and publish the [geometric contract](../adr/0005-eccentric-normal-offset.md).
- Measure PDF table wrapping and continue overflow on numbered pages instead of crossing the footer.

The v1.2 program editor remains deferred.

## Limits

Constant normal thickness is exact in the geometric model; the developed contour is numerical.
The 0.01 mm refinement target is not a certified manufacturing tolerance. Circular end diameters
belong to the selected reference surface; other walls generally have non-circular end curves.
The neutral factor and circular-section springback estimate still require process calibration.
No material deformation, CNC axis positions or collision model is introduced.
